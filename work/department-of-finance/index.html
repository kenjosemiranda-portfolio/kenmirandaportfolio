Making Policy Legible

Public information video and design for the Department of Finance

Organization
Department of Finance, Republic of the Philippines
Role
Communications Specialist
Status
Completed
Sector
Government
Capabilities
  • Public information
  • Explainer video
  • Data storytelling
  • Policy communication

Public information videos and graphics that translate economic data, public debt and environmental policy into something people can follow at a glance — including a Philippine debt explainer and an anti-single-use plastic campaign I directed, wrote, narrated and edited.

The brief

Finance and climate policy matter to everyone, and almost no one reads the reports. The job was to take public debt, labor statistics, investment figures and environmental policy and turn them into public information that holds up to scrutiny and still reads — or plays — in a few seconds on a phone.

Approach

  • Explain, then persuade. The Philippine Debt Explainer walks viewers through a topic most people avoid; the Anti-Single Use Plastic Campaign asks them to change a habit.
  • One idea per graphic. Each piece leads with the single number or statement that matters, then supports it.
  • Show the source. Data graphics carry their source — Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas, UNCTAD, the Global Climate Risk Index — because accuracy is the point of public information.
  • Series, not one-offs. The #ClimateActionPH set explains the Philippines’ positions at COP26 across a run of consistent, linked explainers.

My role

As Communications Specialist, I developed communication materials for government programs and public information campaigns, translating technical and policy information into accessible written and multimedia content.

On the two videos above I was director, writer, narrator and video editor.

Reflection

The work strengthened my ability to translate technical information into accessible communication, in an environment where accuracy and accountability are not optional. It is still the standard I hold my work to: accurate first, then clear, then interesting.
