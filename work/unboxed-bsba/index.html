A case-based learning initiative for DYCI's business administration program. I conceived and led it, and wrote and edited about 100 of its cases. In 2026 it was one of five innovations presented at the 1st Research Impact Conference in Dublin.

The brief

Business students can usually define a concept long before they can use it. Lectures and frameworks cover the what, but rarely the moment when someone has to decide with incomplete information. UNBOXED was built to close that gap for the Bachelor of Science in Business Administration program at Dr. Yanga’s Colleges.

Approach

Teach through cases. Each UNBOXED case places a business concept inside a situation students can recognize, then asks them to read the problem, weigh the options and apply what they have learned. The collection is organized into five categories, supported by a gameplay format and the INTEGRITY decision framework, both explained in the videos.

My background is in marketing and multimedia, so I treated the cases as communication, too: plain language, familiar settings and supporting video, so the material holds a student’s attention long enough to do its work.

My role

  • Proposed the initiative and led its development
  • Authored and edited approximately 100 educational cases across five categories
  • Directed and edited the UNBOXED videos: the explainer, the gameplay, the INTEGRITY decision framework and the Dublin feature

Outcome

  • Launched in 2025 within DYCI’s BSBA program.
  • Selected for the 1st Research Impact Conference, organized by the Academy of Management and the Association to Advance Collegiate Schools of Business (AACSB) at University College Dublin, Ireland, in 2026. UNBOXED was one of five innovations invited, and I presented it.

Reflection

UNBOXED is the clearest example of how I like to work: start from a real need, design the structure, then do the writing myself. It also confirmed something I keep relearning — good learning material is, first of all, good communication.
