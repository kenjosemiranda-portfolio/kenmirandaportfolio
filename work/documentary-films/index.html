Short documentaries on history, heritage and community: PARAYA (1st Runner-up, NCCA CinemaLamrag 2022), the DYCI BINHI Museum film (1st Runner-up, NCCA Museums and Galleries AVP Competition), Panaghoy (official entry, 5th SINEliksik Bulacan DocuFest) and A Human Heart, Always.

The films

Panaghoy: Mga Piping Sigaw sa Bahay na Pula (2025). An official entry to the 5th SINEliksik Bulacan DocuFest, on the theme of trials and resilience during the Second World War. Finalist at the Bulgary Refugee Film Festival, with a Special Citation for Gender Sensitivity and Visual Documentary Project.

DYCI BINHI Museum. 1st Runner-up in the Museums and Galleries AVP Competition of the National Commission for Culture and the Arts.

Paraya (2022). A docufilm on the work of DYCI’s Center for Community Engagement during the COVID-19 pandemic, built around the Filipino value of paraya. 1st Runner-up at CinemaLamrag 2022, National Commission for Culture and the Arts.

A Human Heart, Always. The story of the DYCI Robotics Team, made as the highlight video of the ALAB Museum — the first interactive robotics museum in the Philippines.

My role

Director, writer and video editor on Panaghoy and Paraya.

Recognition

  • 1st Runner-up — CinemaLamrag 2022, NCCA (Paraya)
  • 1st Runner-up — Museums and Galleries AVP Competition, NCCA (DYCI BINHI Museum)
  • Finalist — Bulgary Refugee Film Festival (Panaghoy)
  • Special Citation for Gender Sensitivity and Visual Documentary Project (Panaghoy)

Reflection

Documentary is where my two sides meet: the research and accuracy of public communication, and the craft of making people feel something. Each of these films starts from a real place, institution or community, and tries to do it justice.
