Led the college's multimedia and marketing work — campaigns, publication materials and video — keeping a growing range of programs, events and milestones in one consistent institutional voice. Includes the DYCI profile video, which I directed, wrote, narrated and edited.

The brief

A college speaks to many audiences at once — prospective students and parents, current students, faculty, alumni and the wider community — about programs, milestones, competitions and faith life. The job was to keep all of it recognizably DYCI.

Approach

  • One institutional identity, many registers. Anniversary key visuals, sports features, competition mechanics and seasonal greetings each have their own tone, but share the college’s colors, seal and standards.
  • Content as a system. Recurring formats — program features, calls for entries, event key visuals — make a steady output possible without starting from zero each time.
  • Video alongside print. The DYCI profile and facilities videos carry what a poster can’t: space, movement and atmosphere.

My role

  • As Director, Multimedia and Marketing, led the college’s multimedia and marketing initiatives: creative concepts, visual materials aligned with institutional identity, video and graphic production, and coordination across platforms
  • Directed, wrote, narrated and edited the DYCI Profile video
  • Proposed and implemented the CBA Professional Apprenticeship Program, and edited its video

What was made

Campaign and publication materials for programs, events and milestones; banners and environmental graphics; and institutional video, including the DYCI profile featured above.
