Four programs in development at Golden Donuts, Inc. (Dunkin' Philippines): business permit compliance tracking, corporate mobility, a paper lifecycle program and administrative workflow improvements.

Context

I lead Corporate Administration Services, covering corporate services, workplace operations, administrative systems, compliance coordination and resource optimization. My approach is to translate management priorities into practical programs, clarify responsibilities, strengthen processes and set up the monitoring that supports accountability.

Details here are kept at a high level; no internal data is shown.

The programs

Business permit renewal and compliance tracking. A structured way to monitor permit renewals across company-operated stores: organizing regulatory requirements, identifying prerequisite documents, clarifying which department owns what, and making renewal timelines and compliance status visible. It treats compliance as a shared responsibility of Corporate Administration Services, Technical Services, Operations, Finance and other units.

Corporate mobility optimization. A program to improve transportation access, strengthen controls over official bookings and optimize transportation spending, exploring structured corporate transportation arrangements.

Paper lifecycle program. Better document handling, less unnecessary paper use and more efficient workplace practices.

Administrative systems and process improvement. Developing and refining administrative workflows, documentation procedures and internal coordination, for more consistent processes and clearer accountability.

Status

All four programs are in progress.

Reflection

Even well-designed programs fail without clear responsibilities, appropriate controls, coordination between departments and a way to monitor implementation. I start by understanding how work actually happens now, find where it breaks, and design changes people can adopt and keep.
