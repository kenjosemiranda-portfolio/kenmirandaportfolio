Event videos for Dunkin' Philippines' celebrity fan experiences — Cha Eun-Woo, Byeon Wooseok, TJ Monterde and KZ Tandingan, TXT and ENHYPEN. I directed and edited the 2023–2025 videos and edited the 2026 ones. All are published on Dunkin' PH's official YouTube channel.

The brief

A fan event is over in an afternoon. For most fans, the video is the event — so it has to carry the energy of the day, respect the talent, and still work as brand content for Dunkin’.

My role

  • 2023–2025 — director and video editor: Cha Eun-Woo’s Bundles of CHArm fun meet; TJ Monterde and KZ Tandingan’s Never ‘Bean’ Sweeter fan meet; Byeon Wooseok’s store visit and Daydreamin’ fan meet
  • 2026 — video editor: TXT’s store visit and Wonders at 5:53 fun meet; ENHYPEN’s store visit and fun meet

What was made

Eight event videos, all published on Dunkin’ PH’s official YouTube channel.
